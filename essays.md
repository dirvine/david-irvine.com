## Essays

- **[Curious Minds](/essays/education-or-not/)** — A wandering essay on education and the joy of asking *why*. It begins with what schooling gets wrong, then roams through arithmetic, gravity, zero and infinity, time, truth and consciousness, before proposing a new kind of university that rewards learning rather than certificates. A story of questions with no answers, of answers that question what we think we know, and, mostly, of hope.
